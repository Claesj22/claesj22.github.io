Stenner Eatz is restaurant that captures an off-campus college eating experience. We are a collective of four roommates who enjoy cooking, eating, sharing food, and having a good time. We have the capabilities of constructing high end dishes, but as busy students/young professionals, food isn't always our highest priority. Dinner can be delectible, but sometimes it just needs to be edible. Dine at Stenner Eatz for an authentic college eating experience.

Our target audience ranges from college students looking for a quick, budget friendly meal, to foodies who want to relive their college days.

Our customers may be looking for a cheap quick meal or high end homemade cuisine. Our seating ranges from the living room to the front yard garden.

Dining can be divine, but sometimes it just needs to be good enough
